Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SECURE Measurement Assistant helps users prepare measurement estimates and E-MB valuations. This policy explains how the desktop application handles information.

Information used

The app may use a Google account email address to sign in and manage access. Documents, estimates, measurements, and saved drafts are normally stored on the user’s computer. Payment transactions are processed by PayU; SECURE does not collect or store payment-card or UPI credentials.

Payments and access

The app offers a 15-day trial, a monthly subscription, and a per-task option. Payment status may be verified with the payment provider to activate access. Payment providers process their information under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

SECURE does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed for sign-in, payment confirmation, or services requested by the user.

Security and retention

Users should protect their computer and account credentials. Local documents remain under the user’s control and can be removed by the user. Access records are retained only as needed to provide the service, prevent misuse, and meet applicable obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the SECURE Measurement Assistant publisher through the support information shown in the application and Microsoft Store listing.
